No invoice-to-contract validation

The weak spot
For suppliers under contract, invoice prices, discounts, and payment terms are not checked against the contract at the line level.
The impact
Overpayment, missed discounts, payment terms not enforced
Potential solutions
Connect the CLM to AP and the ERP so invoice lines are matched to contract prices and terms, with exceptions routed to sourcing.
