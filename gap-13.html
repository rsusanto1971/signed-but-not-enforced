Manual renewals and price increases

The weak spot
The CLM sends a renewal notice, but renewals and supplier price-increase requests are handled outside the system, often without checking the contract's price cap.
The impact
Missed notice deadlines, unwanted auto-renewals, price increases above the contract cap
Potential solutions
Use an AI agent to collect the business owner's renewal intent and open a sourcing project early enough to negotiate before the notice deadline.
Check every supplier price-increase request against the contract's cap or price index before anyone accepts it.
For low-value, low-risk categories, pilot automated supplier negotiation within approved limits.
